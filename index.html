The Spark

My passion for technology wasn't born out of abundance, but rather a lack of access. Growing up, I didn't have my own hardware; instead, I spent hours watching friends play games, captivated by the digital world but unable to directly interact with it.

That initial curiosity turned into an absolute obsession after watching Mr. Robot. It wasn't just the hacking that hooked me—it was the profound realization of how deeply interconnected and vulnerable our digital infrastructure is.

That shift from a fascinated observer to an active, hands-on learner defined my path. I went from watching screens over shoulders to diving deep into the technical intricacies of how systems operate—and how they break.

Process & Methodology

I believe in a transparent, methodical approach to offensive security. In my active projects and write-ups, I document exact attack paths, detailing the logic behind each pivot.

01. Recon & Strategy

Mapping the attack surface meticulously. Identifying logical entry points, enumerating subdomains, and understanding the target's unique infrastructure.

02. Execution

Executing targeted exploits. Sharing visual, step-by-step documentation of bypasses, privilege escalation, and lateral movement with screenshots.

03. Mitigation

Translating offensive wins into defensive strategies. I don't just show how I broke in; I share practical, actionable tips on patching the vulnerability.
